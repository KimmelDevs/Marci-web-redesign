Meet Marci

A REALTOR® for nearly three decades

Marci got her start in real estate in 1995 and later became a licensed Broker in Seattle before relocating to enjoy the sunshine of Southern Nevada. Today she leads The Ridge Realty Group in Pahrump, working alongside her daughter to guide clients through every stage of buying and selling.

Her team has ranked among the top residential sales teams in Pahrump for four years running — built on the kind of local knowledge that only comes from living, working, and raising a family in this community.
